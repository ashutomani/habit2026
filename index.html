ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

Connecting Ideas.
Creating Impact.

MNNIT Allahabad Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh, India

The 2nd International Conference on Health and Agricultural Biotechnology: Interdisciplinary Trends (HABIT-2026) will bring together researchers, academicians, scientists, students, industry professionals and innovators to discuss emerging developments at the interface of biotechnology, health, agriculture and allied disciplines.

The conference aims to provide a multidisciplinary platform for exchange of ideas, presentation of innovative research and development of meaningful collaborations. HABIT-2026 will particularly emphasize the integration of modern biotechnology with computational approaches, artificial intelligence, genomics, environmental science and translational research.

The conference will feature expert lectures, keynote addresses, oral and poster presentations, scientific discussions and opportunities for networking among participants from academia, research institutions and industry.

Innovation Emerging technologies and ideas
Collaboration Academia, research and industry
Interdisciplinary Connecting diverse disciplines
CONFERENCE THEMES

Explore the Scientific Tracks

Research papers and abstracts are invited in the following areas, including but not limited to:

01

Health Biotechnology

Biomedical research, therapeutics, diagnostics and translational biotechnology.

02

Agricultural Biotechnology

Crop improvement, plant biotechnology, agricultural genomics and sustainable agriculture.

03

Bioinformatics & Computational Biology

Computational genomics, structural biology, drug discovery and biological data science.

04

AI & Machine Learning

Artificial intelligence applications in healthcare, agriculture and biotechnology.

05

Environmental Biotechnology

Bioremediation, climate change, environmental omics and sustainable technologies.

06

Genomics & Proteomics

Genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics and systems biology.

07

Nanobiotechnology & Green Chemistry

Nanomaterials, nano-biotechnology and environmentally sustainable chemistry.

08

Food, Diet & Nutrition

Food biotechnology, nutrition, nutraceuticals and functional foods.

09

Industrial Biotechnology

Bioprocessing, fermentation, enzymes, biomaterials and biomanufacturing.

10

Biomedical Engineering

Bioelectronics, biomechanics, biomedical devices and engineering applications.

11

IPR, Policy & Ethics

Intellectual property, biotechnology policy, regulation and ethical issues.

12

Interdisciplinary Biotechnology

Innovative research integrating biotechnology with other scientific disciplines.
