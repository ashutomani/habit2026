About HABIT-2026

The 2nd International Conference on “Health and Agricultural Biotechnology: Interdisciplinary Trends (HABIT-2026)” will be held during October 30 – November 01, 2026 at the Department of Biotechnology, Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology Allahabad.

The conference aims to provide a global platform for researchers, academicians, and industry professionals to exchange ideas and showcase cutting-edge research in biotechnology, healthcare, agriculture, and interdisciplinary sciences. Around 500 researchers are expected to attend this event from different parts of the globe to exchange their ideas and innovations with peers.
